# Reduce a product before building

Provenance: synthetic illustration, not real user feedback or measured results.

## Input

“Find a small way to help students learn colour relationships.”

## Mode and process

DISCOVER. Compare an interactive contrast lab, a curated palette archive and a colour-matching game. Each has a different core loop and learning hypothesis.

## Actionable outcome

Recommend a one-screen contrast lab if the learning goal is readable pairs. Omit accounts and AI until evidence supports them. This is a hypothesis, not a research result.

## Verification and limits

Ask learners to complete a defined comparison task with a prototype; record misconceptions and completion behavior rather than inventing retention gains.
